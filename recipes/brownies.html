Brownies

Description

These are the most chocolatley and yummy brownies ever. EVER.

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup white sugar
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2tbsp water
  • 1.5 cups chocolate chips
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 0.5 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2/3 cup flour
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.25 tsp baking soda

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F
  2. Boil sugar, butter and water then add in chocolate chips until melted
  3. Combine flour, salt and baking soda and stir into mixture
  4. Spread batter evenly into greased pan
  5. Bake until top is dry, about 20-30 minutes
  6. Let cool, cut and enjoy!i
